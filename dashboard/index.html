How the ranking is made — model radar-2

One function scores every protocol from the snapshot's figures; no name is special-cased.

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × maturity factor × breadth factor × scale factor

  • Category multiple — what a launched token in this category is worth per $1 of TVL: chains 1.50, prediction markets 1.40, derivatives 1.30, DEXs 1.10, lending 0.85, yield vaults 0.50–0.55, farms 0.45.
  • Growth factor — exp(0.55 × 7-day TVL change), the change clamped to −80%…+200%; unreported growth counts as 0.
  • Maturity factor — 0.55 + 0.45 × (1 − exp(−age / 900 days)): years run with no token count, with diminishing returns; an unknown listing date counts as 540 days.
  • Breadth factor — 1 + 0.055 per doubling of the chain count: more chains, more holders a token can launch into.
  • Scale factor — (TVL / $100M)−0.06: launched tokens trade at lower value-to-TVL ratios the larger the protocol already is.

The board covers the whole watchlist — pre-token protocols at or above the snapshot's $50M floor that are not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades — and adds the other pre-token protocols with the most TVL under the same product rules. Protocols that already have a token are not ranked: their value is a market fact, not a model's claim.
